The Challenge

At Paranjape Autocast Pvt. Ltd., the foundry facility previously relied on manual meter logs taken on physical paper registers by operators walking between sub-meters at each shift change. Peak load demand spikes went unnoticed until monthly utility penalty invoices arrived, leaving management with zero live visibility into departmental consumption or equipment leaks.

The Engineering Approach

TEK10 engineered an automated industrial telemetry pipeline, integrating multi-function energy meters across major plant feeders using RS485 / Modbus protocols wired into an edge IoT gateway. Telemetry is streamed into a high-throughput time-series database and rendered on responsive operational dashboards, permanently eliminating manual meter logbooks.

System Architecture & Tech Stack
Industrial Telemetry Modbus RS485 Python & FastAPI Time-Series Database SVG Data Visualization Automated Shift Reports

Verified Operational Impact

Enabled plant management to track specific energy consumption (kWh/Ton) in real time, pinpointing off-peak heating inefficiencies and replacing manual shift paper logs with automated daily shift reports.

INTELLIGENT MOULD COUNTING

Computer Vision System

A computer-vision solution designed to detect, track, and count casting moulds on moving foundry transfer conveyors using machine learning models and industrial image processing.

The Challenge

In high-temperature foundry environments, manual tallying by operators on the conveyor line led to counting discrepancies between raw poured moulds and final cooled castings, creating inventory mismatches.

The Engineering Approach

We deployed an overhead industrial camera connected to an edge inference processor. Custom object detection models were trained on annotated foundry images across varying ambient lighting and thermal contrast, running real-time tracking with bounding boxes and honest confidence metrics.

System Architecture & Tech Stack
Artificial Intelligence Computer Vision OpenCV & PyTorch Edge Inference Object Detection & Tracking Discrepancy Logging

Verified Operational Impact

Replaced prone manual tally counting with an automated visual audit log, providing real-time production numbers directly to shift managers without requiring manual tally clickers.
